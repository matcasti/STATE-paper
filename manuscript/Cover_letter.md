Dear Editor,

We submit "A Systems Theory of Cardiac-Autonomic Trajectories and Equilibria" as a Hypothesis paper for *BioSystems*.

The paper addresses a basic question of biological organization: how the organization of a regulated system (the stable regimes it can occupy, how it moves between them, and how it recovers from perturbation) can be told apart from the limited signal through which it is observed. We use the autonomic regulation of the heart as a tractable case and propose a theory, STATE, in which regulation is a latent stochastic dynamical system observed only through RR intervals. The theory is stated as four postulates with explicit assumptions and non-assumptions.

The hypothesis is supported by two formal results and a minimal simulation, and it is made testable through four falsifiable hypotheses, each with an operational design and the result that would refute it. The work is theoretical and computational; no empirical data were used. Code and results are openly available at https://github.com/matcasti/STATE-paper.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approve its submission. The authors declare no competing interests.
